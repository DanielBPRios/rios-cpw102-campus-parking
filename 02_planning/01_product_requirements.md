# Product Requirements

**Project:** Campus Parking Helper

**Team member:** Daniel Rios

**Date:** 30 September 2026

## User and problem

Who is the user? Students & Visitors who park on Campus

**What problem does the user have?** The user has problems calculating costs themselves, which leads to (1) potential mistakes and (2) confusion on partial hours counting toward costs.

## Goal

**Our program will help the user:** Calculate the estimate costs of parking before or during their parking session while on campus.

## Requirements

Write three things the program must do. Make each one specific enough to test.

1. The program must calculate with the inclusion of hourly costs, the independent variable.
2. The program must include partial costs alongside the full hours.
3. The program must accurately read the number of hours the car parked.

## Not included

What will this version **not** do?

- calculate beyond the hundreths 
- make transactions for the user
- overcomplicate code by nestling functions within functions too frequently

## Success

We will know the product works when:
- it calculates the estimate costs of parking with both whole and partial hours and communicates to the user of only their parking costs (as per following the goal to its precise intent and adding nothing more).
